---
title: mc-txt
summary: mc-txt is a gamified text generator.
---

mc-txt is a gamified text generator. Based on searches in the Wikipedia API, mc-txt receives extracts related to the provided inputs and uses Markov chains to generate new text.


      The project was developed in the workshop *Writing Like Machines* by Matías Buonfrate and Gerardo Montoya, and included in the [Lit(e)Lat V2.0](https://antologia.litelat.net/volumen2/index.html) anthology.
