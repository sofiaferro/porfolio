mc-txt is a gamified text generator. Based on searches in the Wikipedia API, mc-txt receives extracts related to the provided inputs and uses Markov chains to generate new text.


Like pit0nisa, mc-txt was born in the *Writing Like Machines* workshop (Matías Buonfrate and Gerardo Montoya) and is part of the [Lit(e)Lat V2.0](https://antologia.litelat.net/volumen2/index.html) anthology.
