BudínCam is a home monitoring system built with an ESP32-CAM connected to WiFi and linked to a Telegram bot.

Through commands like `/photo` or `/flash`, it allows remote image capture and control of the built-in flash.

The project was designed to check on my cat when I'm away—especially to ensure her automatic feeder keeps working.
